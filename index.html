Public Transport — Riverlink Corridor

Live Metlink vehicles currently near the corridor, and their estimated performance against timetable. Since Metlink's live feed gives position only (no delay field), this is derived by us: each vehicle's live position is matched against the two nearest scheduled stops on its own trip, interpolated by relative distance, and compared to actual time. Treat as an estimate, not an exact GTFS-RT delay figure. Covers the 7 main routes serving this area (110, 120, 121, 145, 149, 154, N22) - school services excluded.
